Concept lot layouts

Five ways to subdivide the flat field of parcel _3, with the commercial plaza at the road, the gate at the neck, a park, a communal garden and the forest trails behind. Lots priced at US$60 per m² with tiers; infrastructure costed with planning-grade Dominican unit rates.

2. How the layouts were drawn

  • Developable envelope. Only ground under 15 % slope connected to the entrance (8.7 of the 10.1 ha field). The steep north lobe stays as hillside reserve: either future lots on a contour-following street, or forest.
  • Vehicle movement. Streets are 6 m concrete carriageways in an 8 m right of way, laid parallel to the field's long axis (60°) with connectors that close loops; connectors steeper than 15 % are dropped. The single entrance is on Carretera Los Quemados at the south-west tip; the 35-60 m wide neck holds the commercial plaza and parking outside the gate, then the gate and guardhouse where the field opens up. Every lot fronts a street.
  • Buffers and amenities. The commercial plaza takes the neck at the road, on the site of the current family house (dashed blue outline, to be replaced): mini-market, liquor store, café, offices, parking, open to the village. The gate follows, then the family park inside it (fenced playground, dog park, court) as the buffer to the homes. The communal garden reuses a greenhouse in the middle of the field. The clubhouse with pool and mirador terrace sits uphill at the toe of the hill, around 720 m, secluded above the lots with the valley in front and the forest of Parcela 2 behind, reached by its own short lane from the end of the spine; a lined lagoon with a fountain lies just below its terrace. The strip is the trailhead; Parcela 2 is communal forest with trails and the mirador. Option C adds four view lots on the 822 m contour of Parcela 2, served from the road end on the north side (needs the right of way from the road study).
  • Mountain patterns (options D and E). D groups homes into pocket clusters around a shared green on a narrow loop lane the spine runs through, denser by the plaza and looser uphill, with finca lots along the meandering spine between them and a pedestrian and golf-cart path from the park to the greens, garden, clubhouse and trailhead. E adds Parcela 2: two contour "finger" lanes from the north road end, lots single-loaded on the valley side so no roof blocks the view behind, a 14 m step between the two lanes, ravines from the terrain model kept as forested green chutes, each finger ending in a micro cul-de-sac of 4 to 6 estate lots around a small viewpoint pavilion, and a perimeter trail on the contour below the lots so residents walk to the clubhouse without using the lanes. Civil notes: each ridge lot gets a graded zero-step pad from parking to living level (cut and fill under 1.5 m), lanes at 5 m with turnouts, and the north access needs the right of way from the road study.
  • Lot shapes. Streets are laid along the field's axis with a gentle wave (strongest in B, moderate in C, slight in A); lots are cut perpendicular to the curving street so they fan naturally, at roughly 1 wide to 1.4 deep (about 30 by 40 m for villa lots, 38 by 55 m for finca lots). Dead ends get turning circles. Contour lines every 2 m (labels every 10 m) are drawn on the map; lot sizes appear as labels when zoomed in.
  • Pricing tiers. Base US$60/m²; +10 % for the upper part of the field (better views, above 722 m); -15 % for lots with 12-22 % ground slope; +25 % for view lots on Parcela 2.

3. Infrastructure cost model

Planning-grade 2026 unit rates for a small private lotification in the DR interior (contractor quotes will move these by ±30 %): concrete road US$60/m², ditches and culverts US$90/m, underground medium and low voltage US$110/m plus US$18,000 per transformer, LED lighting US$30/m, water main US$32/m with two wells at US$38,000 each and a tank-pump station at US$55,000, gravity sewer US$75/m plus a package treatment plant at US$2,600 per lot (minimum US$90,000), fibre ducts US$18/m, earthworks, gate and guardhouse US$50,000, perimeter fence US$38/m, clubhouse with pool, playground and dog park US$260,000, a 300 m² commercial plaza shell US$165,000, engineering, survey, permits and legal at 10 % plus US$45,000, and 15 % contingency. Excluded: land, marketing and sales commissions (budget 8-10 % of revenue), taxes, house construction.
